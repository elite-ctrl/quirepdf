Quire for Windows

1. Unzip this folder anywhere. A USB stick is fine. Nothing is installed.
2. Double-click "Start Quire.bat".
3. Your browser opens Quire. Leave the black window open while you work.
4. Close that window when you are finished.

Your PDFs and marks stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.
Open the same PDF again on this computer and the marks come back.
